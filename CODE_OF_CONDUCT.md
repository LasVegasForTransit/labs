# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Community standard

### Pledge

Members, contributors, and leaders create a harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Participation supports an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

### Expected behavior

Constructive participation includes empathy, respect for different experiences, clear feedback,
responsibility for mistakes, and attention to the good of the community.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, insults, personal attacks, and the publication of private
information without permission are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

### Scope

The Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever a person officially represents Las Vegans
for Better Transit in public.

### Reports

Private reports go to [hello@lasvegasfortransit.org](mailto:hello@lasvegasfortransit.org). Community
leaders investigate promptly, protect the reporter's privacy and security, and communicate the
result fairly.

### Responses

Leaders remove, edit, or reject participation that violates the community standard. The response
matches the impact, severity, duration, and pattern of behavior:

1. A correction addresses isolated unprofessional or unwelcome behavior.
2. A warning sets explicit boundaries and consequences for repetition.
3. A temporary ban stops interaction for a defined period after a serious violation.
4. A permanent ban follows sustained harassment, aggression, or repeated violations.
