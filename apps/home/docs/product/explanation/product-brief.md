# Product brief

Residents, advocates, public officials, journalists, researchers, and builders use Labs home to
discover LVBT experiments and judge how actively each one is maintained.

## Catalog experience

The opening identifies LVBT Labs, explains why the program exists, and leads directly into real
projects. The explanation is substantive program context rather than a marketing interstitial.

Every entry includes the project name, plain-language summary, kind, lifecycle state, and preview
image. Active work leads the catalog. Deprecated work names its successor, retired work moves into
the archive, and graduated work links to its canonical source without changing the Labs URL.

Filtering appears only when the catalog becomes difficult to scan without it.

The footer carries the LVBT wordmark, organization and involvement directories, the organization
mission statement, and a compact Labs utility row. It does not duplicate the project catalog.

## Ownership boundary

Home consumes validated metadata. It never embeds another lab's business logic, copies project
documentation, or coordinates runtime requests between projects.
