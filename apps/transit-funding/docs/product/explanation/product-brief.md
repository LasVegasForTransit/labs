# Product brief

Southern Nevada residents, advocates, policymakers, journalists, and fiscal analysts use
TransitFunding to inspect the claim that the region cannot afford better public transit.

## Argument and evidence

The publication traces regional transportation revenue, estimates the cost of a useful network, and
lets the reader test policies against the remaining gap. Interactive controls expose the tradeoffs
directly instead of asking readers to accept a conclusion on trust.

Every figure carries its source, URL, retrieval date, unit, and dollar year when applicable.
Estimates include their bounds. Source and license records stay beside the fiscal model.

## Visual language

Color communicates how difficult a funding source is to unlock: already authorized, legislative
action, constitutional change, or federal match. Direct labels identify the source itself, so the
argument never depends on color discrimination.

Band order follows policy difficulty and declaration order rather than value. Readers can change one
input without the rest of the diagram jumping to new positions.
